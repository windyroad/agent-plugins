# Contribute to an outcome

Help achieve the selected outcome through its current increment. Find and own
a useful contribution rather than waiting for a preassigned task.
MUST actively look for useful ways to advance the shared result or make another
contributor's work easier. Do not sit idle waiting for work, and do not make
“Is there anything I can do to help?” your only contribution.

Read the [project tracking and shared contributions](../outcome-led-planning/references/project-tracking.md)
for context selection, identity, authority, reviews, task claims, dependencies,
selected-backend records, communication and human-request preparation. Resolve the saved backend first. Local mode uses its agreed plan and channel without GitHub setup; GitHub mode also reads [the GitHub coordination protocol](../outcome-led-planning/references/github-coordination.md) and invokes the installed [setup-github-tracking skill](../setup-github-tracking/SKILL.md) only when selected setup is missing or incomplete. This skill guides
useful assistance within the existing plan; it can be used on its own.

## Choose the outcome you are helping

Establish whether your contribution is focused on a specified outcome or open to
any eligible outcome under the user's direction or existing contributor/project
guidance. Reuse an assignment or current task's parent chain when it already
identifies the outcome. With open scope, choose an outcome with a concrete useful
need, considering current priorities, capability and authority, and state that
choice to its owner. Do not ask the human to choose when that freedom is already
established. Clarify only genuinely missing scope or conflicting direction.

Then follow that outcome's current Now increment and plan revision. Choosing or
promoting increments belongs to its outcome owner. Bind your contribution to the
selected outcome, current increment/revision and task before claiming work.
Finishing a task permits a fresh outcome choice within standing open scope;
keep focused assignments and existing watched operations with their owners.

## Find a concrete need

On joining or resuming, read the outcome, current increment brief and discussion,
active tasks, dependencies and recent results. Read the current increment discussion regularly during active work, including while coding or watching CI, and before dependent actions, shared edits, pushes and handovers. Follow the agreed bounded active-work cadence; pending agreement does not suspend authorized checks. These are active-work checks, not a persistent poller. Respond to
new information affecting your work; do not wait to be mentioned.

Look for the smallest useful action that helps the current path to done:

- **Direct help:** check a missing piece of evidence, locate a needed reference,
  review a concrete change, prepare a precise handoff, or remove an immediate
  obstacle for another contributor.
- **Indirect help:** improve a known step the increment will actually execute,
  such as a slow CI/CD step, when the benefit is concrete and the change follows
  the applicable ownership, review and delivery rules.

Think of helping someone removing butterfly nuts: offering your cupped hand can
make their work easier without asking them to design a job for you. Small help
counts when it removes real friction. General cleanup, speculative optimization,
extra verification and new coordination machinery are not useful merely because
an agent is available.

## Act within the shared plan

For small, safe assistance already within your contribution and authority, do it
and report the result. You do not need a fresh human approval just to be helpful.
Check current work first so you do not duplicate an operation or invalidate
someone else's evidence.

For each separately owned contribution:

1. Inspect the current Now revision, discussion, dependencies and existing claims.
2. Choose a bounded useful result and announce its scope, acceptance and file or
   operation boundary in the selected backend’s increment discussion. Resolve overlap, shared operations
   or authority questions with the affected owners before acting.
3. Reuse a matching unclaimed task or create one just before work. In local mode,
   record bounded scope, owner, revision and dependencies in the agreed local plan.
   In GitHub mode, apply the established task-kind marker and native parent and
   genuine dependencies. Add it to a Project only when selected or required.
   Claim with the actual agent/session, read back saved records and recheck overlap.
4. Execute within the assessed plan and authority, keeping evidence and status
   current. Small routine steps stay in the task. In GitHub mode, follow the
   [work checkpoints](../outcome-led-planning/references/github-coordination.md#reconcile-tracking-at-work-checkpoints)
   and MUST use the [executable checkpoint](../outcome-led-planning/references/github-project-checkpoint.md)
   for selected-Project join/resume reconciliation. Read back issue and selected Project changes before dependent work.
5. Record the verified result and finish, or release with a handover stating
   evidence, blocker and next step when no useful authorized work remains.
   After those updates, verify the saved task and affected increment records
   and applicable selected Project fields before reporting that transition current
   or choosing more work. Read back later handoff/next-action writes too; earlier
   task-only verification does not verify those increment updates. Reuse
   still-valid matching field readbacks.
6. Choose and document the next useful contribution from current evidence, then
   repeat the claim checks before starting it.

A non-overlapping contribution within the assessed Now does not need an owner
allocation or new human approval. The owner coordinates acceptance, sequence and
integration; explicit assignments and handoffs remain valid. Hold only one
contribution claim at a time. Never take another agent's claim, widen access,
bypass review, change the accepted plan unilaterally or repeat CI/provider
operations to fill a wait. Assignment does not grant authority.

While waiting, keep ownership of an operation you must watch and handle. Look for
non-conflicting assistance within that contribution; do not abandon the watcher
or claim a second task. If genuinely blocked with no useful authorized next step,
record the exact blocker and unlock condition and release the reservation under
the shared rules. Do not manufacture work to retain an In progress status.

The planner MUST NOT add speculative risk-mitigation tasks. Apply scorer findings based on the applicable policy and risk register; availability is not a reason to invent precautionary work.

## Coordinate and report honestly

Use the selected backend’s increment discussion for offers with a concrete result, assignments,
acknowledgements, handovers and useful findings. Say what you did, what it enables,
and what remains uncertain. Keep the task brief and any selected Project fields aligned
with verified work. Record material results, not repeated no-change heartbeats.

A posted request or mention does not wake an idle agent or prove acceptance.
Distinguish proposed, assigned, acknowledged, underway and verified work. If the
recipient needs resuming, state that boundary; do not claim they are working.

The **increment owner** keeps acceptance, sequence and integration clear, exposes
useful opportunities and resolves overlap and dependencies. A starting battle
plan is provisional work and dependency guidance, not a reserved backlog. Agents
create/reuse and claim tasks just in time; the owner need not allocate every
contribution. Explicit assignments still require verified availability and
acknowledgement. Before declaring the increment complete, its owner obtains the
[independent acceptance check](../outcome-led-planning/references/planning-details.md#independent-increment-acceptance)
through the existing planning critic, including repair and recheck. Individual
task completion alone does not trigger that increment-level check. Preserve other contributors' claims; release your own exhausted claim through the handover and readback rules above. If no useful action is available,
state the concrete limit instead of inventing busywork or asking the human to
manage routine allocation.

Before assigning or presenting work to a human, apply the shared rules'
cog-a11y assessment: explain plainly what will happen, what the human must do,
and why their involvement is necessary. Existing authority still applies; do not
invent an approval request for work the agents are already authorized to do.
