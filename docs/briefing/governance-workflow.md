- **The documented recovery for a false upstream detection reuses the real hold marker's own prefix, so applying it hides the ticket from the loop.** When a ticket's Root Cause Analysis trips the strict external-root-cause token scan (the literal words `upstream` / `third-party` / `external` / `vendor`, or any scoped npm package name), the transition skill's recovery path tells you to append `- **Upstream report pending** -- false positive; detection misfire`. That marker is also the substrate `/wr-itil:work-problems` Step 2.4 gate (0) reads to classify a ticket non-dispatchable — and the gate clause matches the prefix with **no misfire exclusion**. Applying the documented recovery therefore drops the ticket out of the loop entirely: it no longer blocks `ALL_DONE` and is never re-selected. Hit live on 2026-09-19 by P441, a ticket about the loop's own selection filter, whose body trips the scan only because it *discusses* upstream-blocked tickets as one of the classes it must recognise. Record the misfire under a different bold label (`- **External-root-cause detection misfire** --`) so the prefix does not open a line, or the ticket makes itself invisible to the machinery it exists to fix. The gate clause needs the same NOT-misfire guard; tracked on P441.

- **A missing cadence is not always missing wiring — check what the loop is *permitted* to fire before you wire it.** Every auto-fired pre-flight the AFK loop runs is read-only by construction: the inbound-discovery refresh and the outbound-responses poll both only read. The one reconciliation that would have to *write* to an external party — the batch lifecycle catch-up that posts owed updates to reporters and closes their issues — was ratified as **user-initiated only, never auto-fired** (the reporting contract's 2026-06-18 batch-mode amendment; the dispatching skill repeats the clause verbatim). So "the scanner exists, the dispatcher exists, just add a Step 0x pre-flight" reads as a two-hour wiring job and is actually a reversal of ratified substance that needs a superseding decision. The tell is the direction of the side effect: a cadence that only reads is framework-resolved and you may build it; a cadence that speaks to real people on the project's behalf is a maintainer's call every time. Hit live 2026-09-19 working the inbound fix-released verdict ticket, where the scanner reported 24 owed updates and the correct move was to queue the question, not the pre-flight.
