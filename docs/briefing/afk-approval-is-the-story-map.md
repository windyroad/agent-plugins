## What You Need to Know

### Render a story map AFTER the story transition, never before (2026-09-19)

`wr-itil-render-story-map` resolves each card's status chip and both hrefs from filesystem truth at render time. Render before a paired `git mv` of the story file and the card is stale by construction: it keeps `data-status="draft"` and links to `stories/draft/STORY-NNN-*.md`, a path the story has already left, while the stories README written in the same change asserts the new status. The two disagree on one fact, and the dead link is what the "first not-done story" traversal follows. Nothing at commit time detects it — the risk scorer caught it at 10/25 High on the P543 iteration only because the diff was read. The ordering is the whole control: `git mv` and edit the frontmatter first, then render, then stage both together. It bites once per lifecycle hop, so expect it again at accepted → in-progress. Re-rendering is self-correcting, and the map's ratification is untouched either way — rows and cards sit outside the oversight basis (ADR-103). <!-- signal-score: 2 | last-classified: 2026-09-19 | first-written: 2026-09-19 -->

### A ratified map lets one unattended iter run the whole thing: ticket, vehicle, code, commit (2026-09-19)

P472 is the cleanest witness so far that the hold is genuinely gone when the map is ratified. One `claude -p` iteration took an Open ticket to Known Error with a filled root cause, drew a release row on the already-ratified problem-to-resolution map under an existing activity column, authored the row's story and advanced it to `accepted`, then implemented, tested and committed the code fix — three commits, no human event anywhere. The story was born approved because the only map it named was ratified, and drawing the row plus the card left that map's stored fingerprint validating.

Two things made it cheap. The fix needed no new ADR, so ADR-103's fourth leg never fired. And the vehicle sat on an existing map under an existing column, so none of the other three fired either. Budget was 3 architect spawns, 2 JTBD, 3 risk pipeline, 2 external-comms — the architect rounds, not the authoring, were the cost.

Order matters: the architect's *third* round was spent entirely on "this has no vehicle", after two rounds had already converged the fix's substance. Had the ticket, the row and the story landed first, that round would not have existed. **Transition the ticket and draw the vehicle BEFORE the first architect spawn, not after** — the reviewer checks ADR-071/073/089/096 against what is on disk, and prose promising to author it later does not satisfy them.

- **THE WALL IS BROKEN — approval is the story MAP's (2026-08-07).** ADR-103 retired the ADR-101 carve-out entirely: `wr-itil-check-afk-accept-eligible`, the `afk_accept_pure_decomposition` config key and the `afk-accept:` story field are GONE — do not reach for them. A story is approved when the story map it sits on is ratified. A story carries NO oversight field at all — approval is derived from its `story-maps:` field, and the marker writer refuses a story path. Ratifying a map approves every story on it, including stories added later, so an AFK loop that needs to land a story on an already-ratified map may accept and implement it with no further ratification event. Adding a release row or a story to a row no longer drifts a map's fingerprint. What still needs a human — all four legs, per ADR-103 line 47: a NEW map, a NEW activity column, a change to a map's own prose or traces, **or a NEW ADR**. The ADR leg is the one this entry used to omit and the one that most often fires: if the fix changes substance recorded in a ratified decision, ADR-116 allows change only by supersession, so the proposal needs a new ADR and implementation is refused however well-approved the story is. **Ratification is a MARKER, not a directory** — on 2026-08-21 all five maps sat in `docs/story-maps/draft/` and every one carried `"humanOversight": "confirmed"` with a matching hash. Reading the directory says nothing; read the marker. Test with `story_is_approved` for a story and `is_story_map_ratified` for a map; the story tier has no marker to read. **Everything below still holds for a story whose map is not ratified.** <!-- signal-score: 11 | last-classified: 2026-08-30 | first-written: 2026-07-26 -->
