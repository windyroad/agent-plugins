## What You Need to Know

Older witnesses are in
[`afk-ratification-hold-witnesses-archive.md`](./afk-ratification-hold-witnesses-archive.md)
(P376, P430, P431, P438, P417, P424, P450, P433 — rotated 2026-07-26).

- **P425, 2026-07-26 — eighth witness; cheapest hold to date (3 reviewer spawns + 1 risk-scorer, ONE artefact, ONE commit) because the actionable part was expressible as a doc change and the fix vehicle already existed.** Two structural reasons the bill was this small, both reusable. **First: check whether an existing RFC is the vehicle before budgeting for artefact authoring.** P425 is a defect in a guard that RFC-010 shipped, operating outside a premise RFC-010 itself recorded in its Scope — so P371's existing-vehicle-untraced path applied, and the whole cost was one frontmatter array plus one appended Phase 2 section. No new RFC, no story map, no story: none of `capture-rfc`, the `.html` accessibility gate, or ADR-095/090/096 ever fired. The architect confirmed on grounding stronger than the prompt offered — the RFC's `status:` is `proposed` and never reached `accepted`, so *"Implementation status: COMPLETE"* is body prose, not a lifecycle state, and reopening scope on it reverses no transition. **Second: this hold sits at the option-pick tier, like P450 — one tier deeper than the story gate — and the same discipline applies.** The pick determines the acceptance criteria almost entirely, so authoring the story would breach ADR-095 (placeholder criteria) or ADR-074/P315 (criteria built on an unratified pick); the architect ruled the back-fill *"not merely deferrable, it is correctly blocked"*. Name the full blocked chain in the RFC (ratify pick → capture story on a map → ratify map/story → promote to `accepted`), not just the back-fill, or the empty `stories:` array reads as sloppiness. **The expensive lesson, and it is the one to carry: the architect caught two stale-governance claims I would otherwise have committed as established fact.** I asserted that ADR-052's `structural-permitted` carve-out justified skipping a behavioural test, and that the promptfoo harness for this agent did not exist yet. Both were false and both were one grep from being checked — ADR-052 line 133 *repealed* both escape hatches on 2026-06-09 and line 187 lists the very test I cited as a P290-tracked violation, and `packages/architect/agents/eval/promptfooconfig.yaml` has existed for a month carrying two `[Unratified Dependency]` cases. The real gap was narrower and already ticketed: the positive-fire fixture is missing because `run-agent-eval.sh` line 39 does `cd "$REPO_ROOT"`, so the half-built fixture corpus is never the cwd (P324 / RFC-012). **Before citing a carve-out or asserting a harness gap as your reason for not testing, grep the ADR and `ls` the eval dir.** A repealed permission recited from memory reads exactly like a live one, and asserting a blocker that does not exist is the same class as the memory note on probing tooling before declaring it unavailable.
