→R1 Core loop the spine
  • Capture the problem in seconds, mid-flow Value: noticing costs nothing, so I actually record it instead of carrying it in my head. Traces: STORY-018
  • Find root cause and workaround, and reach Known Error Value: the ticket states why it happens, not just that it does. Traces: STORY-019
  • Start the job's story map, creating it if the job has none Value: the fix is placed in a journey before it is broken into pieces. Traces: STORY-020
  • Add the fix's new stories to the job's story map Value: the map stays the single picture of the work, rather than drifting behind it. Traces: STORY-021
  • Ratify the map and its stories after any change, before building on them Value: I approve the substance once, and the approval is invalidated if the substance moves. Traces: STORY-022, ADR-090
  • Create the RFC, traced to the problem, listing only ratified stories Value: nothing gets built on a story I have not agreed to. Traces: STORY-015
  • Work the RFC's stories one at a time Value: the coordination is in the plan, so the building is ordinary. Traces: STORY-026
  • Ship, verify, and close the problem with a real trace Value: the adopter gets the fix and the record shows how it got there. Traces: STORY-023
◇R2 Refine later phases
  • Reuse stories already on the map when the fix touches existing ones Value: the map accumulates a picture of the product rather than a pile of per-fix stubs. Traces: STORY-024
  • Slice the fix's stories into releases, smallest useful first Value: something shippable comes out early instead of everything landing at once. Traces: STORY-025
  • Leave the backlog draining while I am away Value: I can walk away and the queue still moves. Traces: STORY-040
◇R2 Inbound someone else reported it
  • Capture a problem reported through an inbound channel Value: someone else's report enters the same pipeline as my own observations. Traces: STORY-027
  • Acknowledge the report when it is captured Value: the reporter knows it landed, immediately, without waiting for a fix. Traces: STORY-028
  • Triage the report: accept it, elicit a new job, or decline it Value: a clear disposition beats silence, including when the answer is no. Traces: STORY-032
  • Share the workaround with the reporter Value: they get relief before they get a fix. Traces: STORY-029
  • Tell the reporter a fix is underway Value: the gap between report and release stops feeling like being ignored. Traces: STORY-030
  • Tell the reporter it is released, verify, and close the loop Value: the report ends in a confirmed outcome rather than trailing off. Traces: STORY-031
