What this map traces to

Persona: plugin-user — an adopter with low context on this repository's internals, who is not expected to read our ADRs or patch a cached plugin.

Jobs mapped: JTBD-303 (generated content respects my conventions) is the spine. JTBD-003 (compose only the guardrails I need) governs the choosing and the blocking. JTBD-302 (trust the README describes what I installed) governs the reading. JTBD-101 covers the plugin-developer side.

Problems this closes: P424 — generated content carries characters an adopter's own policy hook rejects, re-introduced on every regeneration. P435 — a gate hardcoded to this repository's shape blocks adopters with no way through.

Decisions the journey rests on: None yet.

Open questions: Widened on 2026-08-05 from a single-story stub into the adopter's whole journey; the prior map held one card about generated characters. Three activities carry no story. The blocking card in D also appears on STORY-MAP-013, seen there from the author's side.
