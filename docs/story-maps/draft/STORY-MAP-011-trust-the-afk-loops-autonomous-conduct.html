What this map traces to

Persona: developer — starts an unattended run and is not watching it. plugin-developer secondary, for the handover activities.

Jobs mapped: JTBD-006 (progress the backlog while I am away) is the spine. JTBD-002 (ship with confidence) governs what the loop is allowed to assert. JTBD-001 (enforce governance without slowing down) governs the close. JTBD-011 (a correction to conduct holds everywhere) governs the handover.

Problems this closes: P430 (correction nudge over-fires), P431 (a decline is not remembered), P433 (a close strands its sibling family), P434 (tickets assert untested claims), P438 and P439 (handovers the developer cannot answer from a phone; reviewers reading stale versions).

Decisions the journey rests on: ADR-101 (an AFK loop may accept and implement a story that only decomposes already-ratified substance).

Open questions: Consolidated on 2026-08-05, absorbing four single-story stub maps — STORY-MAP-005 (correction signal), 007 (handovers), 009 (sibling family) and 010 (verified claims), now archived; each held one card, and all four cards sit on this journey. STORY-048 also appears on STORY-MAP-004, seen there from the reporter's side. Two cells carry no story yet: how the loop explains its own selection, and whether what it writes reads as human prose.
