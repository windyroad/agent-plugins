Trust the AFK loop's autonomous conduct

A story map for the developer who starts /wr-itil:work-problems and walks away. Each rib is one thing the unattended loop must get right for its output to be trustworthy: my corrections land as real records, the tickets it writes say only what was verified, and a close doesn't quietly strand related work. Read the ribs top to bottom as the journey from "the agent noticed" to "the record it left is sound." Each slice links to its story; the backbone, ribs, and slices are refined at the /wr-itil:manage-story-map accepted transition.

Capture my corrections faithfully

Activity A: when I tell the agent it got something wrong, that becomes a durable record; when I didn't, nothing fires.
The correction offer fires only when I actually corrected the agent, not on the framework's own iteration promptsValue: a rare, pointed signal I can trust, instead of a nudge on nearly every autonomous iteration.STORY-047, P430 (inbound #257), RFC-050

Say only what was verified

Activity B: a ticket is read downstream as ground truth, so an untested claim must not wear the same authority as a confirmed finding.
A captured ticket tests its claims against the tree and labels the ones it could not run as guessesValue: no phantom tickets, no fix aimed at a problem that a quick grep would have disproved.STORY-053, P434 (inbound #202/#339), RFC-057

Close without stranding the family

Activity C: when one ticket closes, the still-open siblings in its friction family must not silently fall out of sync.
Before a close lands, the agent shows me the still-open siblings in the same familyValue: related work stays coordinated; a close never quietly orphans its siblings.STORY-052, P433 (inbound #187), RFC-056

This map consolidates three per-fix stub maps (STORY-MAP-005 correction-signal, STORY-MAP-009 sibling-family, and STORY-MAP-010 verified-ticket) into one developer-conduct journey per P473 — the honest unit is the journey, not the single fix. Each rib grows more slices as further conduct fixes drive stories rather than minting sibling maps. Landing order is governed by ADR-101 (an AFK loop may accept and implement a story that only decomposes already-confirmed substance): STORY-047's parents are all confirmed, so it lands first; the others land as their traces confirm.
