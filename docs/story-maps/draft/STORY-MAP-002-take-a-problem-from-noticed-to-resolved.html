Take a problem from noticed to resolved

A story map for the developer who has hit a problem and has to see it through. Read across the top for the journey — notice and capture it, find the cause, decompose the fix, build it, land it. Fixing is one step of five, not the whole job: most of what this journey costs is everything around the fix. Read down the rows for the releases. Each row is one set of stories that ships together, which is what an RFC is — a row that a problem has proposed carries an RFC id, and a row nobody has asked for yet is speculative. Two rows are delivered, two are proposed against a problem, and one is drawn but speculative.
